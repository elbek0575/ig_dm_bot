Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Elektron Hisob ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when people contact our Instagram account or Facebook Page.

Our Instagram messaging automation is used only to send an automatic reply to people who message our business account.

Information We Receive

When you send a message to our Instagram account or Facebook Page, we may receive limited information provided by Meta, such as:

  • your Instagram or Facebook account identifier;
  • your public profile name or username, if available;
  • the message event needed to send an automatic response.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through this automation.

How We Use Information

We use the information only for the following purposes:

  • to automatically reply to incoming Instagram or Facebook messages;
  • to continue communication with users who contact our business;
  • to maintain basic business records of customer communication when needed.

Automatic Replies

If you send a message to our Instagram account, our system may automatically send you a prepared response with business contact information or instructions for continuing the conversation.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your message information with third parties for advertising or marketing resale.

Your information may be processed through Meta platforms, such as Instagram and Facebook, according to Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

Data Storage

Our automation is designed to send a reply and does not require long-term storage of message content. If we keep business communication records, we keep them only as long as reasonably needed for customer service, business operations, or legal requirements.

Data Deletion Requests

You may contact us to request deletion of information related to your communication with our business. We will review and respond to deletion requests within a reasonable time.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by our automation. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for general business communication and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
